About Me

BSc. Computer Science, UDS Nyankpala

I wear multiple hats, but they all share a common goal: communication and impact.

I'm a Computer Science student, Front-End Developer, Student Journalist, Public Speaker, and Mental Health Advocate pursuing my degree at the University for Development Studies (UDS), Nyankpala Campus — passionate about using technology, communication, and creativity to make a meaningful impact.

As a Student Journalist, I'm dedicated to storytelling and uncovering the narratives that shape my community. That same drive to share information led me to mental health advocacy, where I work to break stigma and give a platform to voices that often go unheard.

Parallel to my media work, I'm a Front-End Developer. I view code as another form of storytelling — building accessible, clean, and engaging digital spaces that help people find the information and support they need.

"I'm a developer who builds, a journalist who tells stories, and an advocate who believes people deserve to be heard."
